brand accent. Do not derive the brand from client logos or generic Office theme
colors.

## III. Typography

| Role | Family | Weight | Provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| title | IBM Plex Sans KR | 700 | fact — dominant product-story family |
| body | IBM Plex Sans KR | 400 | fact — dominant product-story family |
| optional cover | Noto Sans KR | 700 | fact — P01 cover title |
| optional English word | IBM Plex Serif | italic | fact — P01 `Carry` only |

Use one core family for a new project. Check local fonts and record a fallback
if needed. A direct-PPTX route keeps its source fonts. Font files are not
embedded in exported decks.

## IV. Logo

No verified standalone INNerCarry logo asset was extracted. P01 combines an
editable wordmark label with a small blue rounded-square image. Recreate that
treatment only when appropriate; do not describe it as the official logo file.

## V. Voice & Tone

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Formality | professional and clear |
| Person | neutral, with concise user-benefit headlines |
| Emoji | absent in the source presentation style |
| Abbreviations | define domain terms when the current audience needs them |
| Copy | one claim per slide, supported by current evidence |

## VI. Icon Style

Use simple flat symbols for actual roles, steps or states. Icons are optional;
source product screenshots and external logos are not bundled as brand assets.
